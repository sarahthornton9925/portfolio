Hello! My name is Sarah Thornton.

I'm a senior at Northwestern University pursuing a B.A. in Psychology and the Segal Design Certificate, inspired by the intersection of human behavior and design.

I'm currently working at Andalusia Design, a custom parametric design and architectural fabrication startup in Austin, TX, where I'm learning how thoughtful design shapes the way people experience spaces.

My goal is to create human-centered designs that make people feel understood and genuinely comfortable in the spaces they move through.
